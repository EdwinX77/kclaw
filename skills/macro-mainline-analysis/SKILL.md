# Macro Mainline Analysis

Use this skill for macro, industry, theme, and sentiment research that must connect events to securities-market implications.

## Workflow

1. Pin the time window and market region.
2. Group facts into policy, rates, FX, commodities, credit, earnings, geopolitics, and sentiment.
3. Identify the dominant mainline and secondary counterforces.
4. Map macro drivers to asset classes, sectors, and styles.
5. Record uncertainty and scenarios that would invalidate the mainline.

## Standards

- Do not overfit single headlines. Require price, flow, or policy confirmation when possible.
- Distinguish first-order impact from second-order read-through.
- Use neutral language when the evidence is mixed.
- Preserve source names and timestamps for later memory review when available.
