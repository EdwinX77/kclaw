# Gmail Daily Review

Use this skill to prepare long-form daily review drafts that can be archived by Gmail or reused by market-memory.

## Structure

1. Executive summary.
2. China market close review.
3. US/EU close and overnight read-through.
4. Macro mainline and sector/theme rotation.
5. Strategy signals and backtest notes.
6. Risk ledger and invalidation conditions.
7. Questions for the next session.

## Writing Rules

- Write for archival clarity, not chat brevity.
- Preserve dates, market session, source names, and known data gaps.
- Distinguish formal signals from interpretation.
- Avoid raw credentials, auth details, hidden config, and private runtime paths.
- End with memory candidates: durable facts, hypotheses to revisit, and resolved/unresolved cases.
