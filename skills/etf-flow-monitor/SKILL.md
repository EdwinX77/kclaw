# ETF Flow Monitor

Use this skill when the task asks for ETF flow, market breadth, index structure, sector rotation, or cross-market risk context.

## Method

- Identify the market and trading session first.
- Separate ETF primary-market flow, secondary-market price/volume behavior, index breadth, and sector/theme rotation.
- Treat large inflows into falling prices or large outflows from rising prices as divergence that needs explanation, not as an automatic signal.
- Compare flows with rates, FX, commodities, major index futures, and news catalysts when relevant.
- Keep conclusions probabilistic and mark data gaps clearly.

## Output

- Breadth: advancing/declining structure and concentration.
- Flow: major ETF/sector inflow and outflow areas.
- Rotation: confirmed leaders, fading leaders, and candidate reversals.
- Risk: liquidity stress, crowding, event exposure, and next-session watch points.
